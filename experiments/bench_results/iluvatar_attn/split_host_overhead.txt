Per attention layer.  issue = CPU time to enqueue (no sync);
gpu = CUDA event time.  A decode step runs 42 such layers.
triton/split call the kernels directly; eng-tri/eng-spl go through the
plugin wrapper and its dispatch gate, which is what the engine pays.
